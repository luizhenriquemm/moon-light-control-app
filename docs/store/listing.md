## English — en-US

**Name:** Moon Light Control

**Short description (66):**
Control your MoonLightControl DMX lighting from a tablet or phone.

**Full description:**

Moon Light Control is the app for MoonLightControl, a DMX lighting server that runs on a Raspberry Pi or mini PC on your local network. It opens your server's console full screen, ready for the service, the rehearsal or the show.

• Recent servers: one tap and you are in. Your login is remembered.
• Find servers: discovers MoonLightControl servers on the Wi-Fi.
• Full screen, and the screen stays on while the console is open.
• Multi-touch faders, scenes, fixtures, universes and shows: everything your server's console offers.
• Export and import shows.
• HTTPS with the server's own certificate: confirm it once, and the app warns you if it ever changes.
• Works without internet, on an isolated lighting network.

Important: the app needs a MoonLightControl server on the same network. It does not control fixtures by itself.

Privacy: no data collection, no ads, no internet use. Everything stays between your device and your server.

Free software (GNU GPL v3 or later).
